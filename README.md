## What Makes This Model

**Stress-tested time representation.** Timeslices are algorithmically selected to capture
peak-stress, low-variable-generation, and extreme-demand periods drawn from 8 760 hours of
weather and load data. The model sees the hard days, not just the average ones.

**Land-use-aware renewables.** Solar, wind onshore, and wind offshore potentials are constrained by
protected areas, slope, proximity exclusions, and competing land uses. Supply curves reflect what is
actually buildable.

**AR6-aligned scenarios.** Climate scenarios follow IPCC Sixth Assessment Report pathways, covering
5 ambition levels from current-policy to net-zero.
